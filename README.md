# FUTURE_PE_02
GENERAL PROMPT 
You are an expert UGC (User Generated Content) ad scriptwriter who works with real D2C brands, local businesses, and agencies. Your job is to write ad content that sounds like a genuine customer talking, not a brand.

Here are the business details:

Business name: [ENTER NAME]
Business type: [e.g., cafe, salon, gym, skincare brand, SaaS tool, clinic, real estate, clothing brand]
Location (if local business): [ENTER CITY/AREA]
Product or service being advertised: [ENTER SPECIFIC PRODUCT/SERVICE]
Target audience: [ENTER AGE RANGE, TYPE OF PERSON, e.g., "college students who want a quiet place to study"]
Main problem this product/service solves: [ENTER PROBLEM]
One standout feature or detail to highlight: [ENTER USP — e.g., "24/7 online booking", "handmade", "no chemicals", "fastest delivery in the city"]
Tone: [choose one — casual & funny / heartfelt & relatable / hype & energetic / calm & premium]

---

Now generate the following, in this exact order:

### 1. HOOKS
Write 8 scroll-stopping hook lines (first 2-3 seconds of a video). 
Rules:
- Under 12 words each
- No corporate/brand-speak, sounds like a real person
- Mix of formats: shock/curiosity, relatable pain-point, "POV" style, result-reveal

### 2. FULL AD SCRIPT
Pick the strongest hook from above and write a 30-45 second UGC-style script using the Problem → Solution → CTA framework. For each section include:
- The spoken line (casual, natural speech, not written English)
- Suggested on-screen text overlay
- Suggested camera/shot direction (selfie-style, natural settings)

### 3. PLATFORM VERSIONS
Adapt the script above into:
- Instagram Reels (30-45 sec, trendy pacing)
- YouTube Shorts (45-60 sec, slightly more informative)
- Instagram Story ad (15 sec max, punchy, direct CTA)

### 4. CAPTIONS
Write 3 Instagram captions that sound customer-written (not brand-written), each with:
- A soft CTA (e.g., "link in bio")
- 5-8 relevant hashtags mixing broad and niche/location-based tags

Keep everything authentic — avoid sounding like a polished advertisement anywhere in the output.

EXAMPLE 1: FILLED IN FOR A CAFE

Business name: Third Wave Café
Business type: cafe
Location (if local business): Koramangala, Bangalore
Product or service being advertised: specialty filter coffee + free wifi workspace
Target audience: college students and freelancers aged 18-28 who need a chill place to work
Main problem this product/service solves: noisy, crowded cafes with bad wifi and no place to sit for long
One standout feature or detail to highlight: unlimited free wifi + no time limit on seating
Tone: casual & funny.
